# Zillow Home Rent Index Analysis (2000–2025)

This project analyzes long-term U.S. rent trends using the Zillow Observed Rent Index (ZORI), focusing on rent growth, volatility, and post-pandemic recovery patterns across all 50 states.
It complements the Zillow Home Value Index project by exploring how rent dynamics reflect broader housing market cycles and regional economic strength.

## Executive Summary

Between 2015 and 2025, U.S. rent markets show strong divergence between high-growth Western states and stable Midwestern and Southern markets.
Key findings highlight patterns of growth acceleration, volatility, and recovery following the COVID-19 shock.

## Key Analyses

### 1. Year-over-Year Rent Growth by State (2016–2025)

Rent growth surged in 2021–2022, especially in Florida, Arizona, and Idaho, exceeding +10–15% YoY during the post-COVID rebound.

2023–2025 show normalization across most states, though Western markets remain above pre-pandemic averages.

**Insight:** Rent growth peaked nationwide in 2022, driven by migration and housing shortages, before stabilizing toward 2025.

### 2. Top & Bottom 5 States by Total Rent Growth (2015–2025)

Colorado (+117%) and Idaho (+99%) led the nation with rents more than doubling.

Louisiana (+14%) and Wisconsin (+15%) showed the slowest increases, reflecting stable but slower-moving markets.

**Insight:** Western states benefited from population inflows and strong economic activity, while the Midwest and South maintained steady, affordable rental levels.

### 3. Rent Volatility (2015–2025)

Montana (11.8%), Vermont (8.3%), and Wyoming (6.7%) recorded the highest rent volatility.

Ohio (1.8%), Missouri (1.9%), and Louisiana (2.1%) showed the most stable rent growth patterns.

**Insight:** High volatility in Western states reflects speculative rent surges and pandemic-driven migration, while Midwest markets demonstrate resilient stability.

### 4. Rent Growth Consistency Index (2015–2025)

Measured as average YoY growth ÷ volatility.

Most consistent: Rhode Island (2.86), Alabama (2.48), and Colorado (2.40).

Least consistent: Vermont (0.17) and West Virginia (0.25).

**Insight:** Balanced markets like Rhode Island and Alabama achieved stable, sustainable rent growth — ideal for long-term investment resilience.

### 5. Rent Recovery Post-COVID (2020–2023)

Montana (+68.7%) led all states in rent recovery, followed by Florida (+38.9%) and Georgia (+34.2%).

Recovery was strongest in inland and Southeastern states, while Northeastern regions showed slower, steadier rebounds.

**Insight:** Post-pandemic migration toward affordable, less dense states accelerated rent recovery in the Mountain West and Southeast.

## Recommendations for Stakeholders

### For Real-Estate Companies & Developers:
Focus on high-growth, moderate-volatility states (e.g., Colorado, Georgia, North Carolina) for sustainable project planning.

### For Investors:
Diversify portfolios by balancing volatile high-return markets (Montana, Idaho) with stable yield markets (Ohio, Alabama).

### For Policymakers & Urban Planners:
Support affordable housing expansion in high-volatility regions to prevent rent over-inflation and migration pressure.

**Source:** Zillow Observed Rent Index (ZORI)
