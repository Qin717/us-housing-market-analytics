# 🏠 U.S. Housing Market Analytics (2000–2025)

This portfolio explores **25 years of U.S. housing and rental market dynamics** using Zillow Research data.  
It combines real-estate, economic, and data-analytics insights to uncover how values and rents evolved before, during, and after major market cycles — including the 2008 crisis and COVID-19 recovery.

---

## 📁 Project Overview

### [01_Zillow_Home_Value_Index_Analysis](01_zillow_home_value_index_analysis/)
Analysis of the **Zillow Home Value Index (ZHVI)** from 2000–2025  
- Identifies long-term **value appreciation trends** by state and region  
- Highlights **crisis impact, recovery speed, and volatility patterns**

### [02_Zillow_Rent_Value_Index_Analysis](02_zillow_rent_value_index_analysis/)
Analysis of the **Zillow Observed Rent Index (ZORI)** from 2000–2025  
- Measures **rent growth, volatility, and consistency** across 50 states  
- Evaluates **post-COVID rent recovery** and **regional resilience**

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Western & tech-driven states** (CA, CO, HI, WA) lead in growth but face **high volatility**  
- **Midwestern & Southern markets** (OH, AL, TN) show **strong stability** and sustainable rent patterns  
- Both markets reveal **cyclical resilience** — fast rebounds after economic downturns
